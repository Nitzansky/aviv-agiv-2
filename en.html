About

In the moments that define you — communication is everything.

Aviv Agiv is a strategic communications advisor and crisis-management expert. For over a decade he has guided leading companies, executives and public figures through the moments that define them.

His work sits at the intersection of message, media and momentum — across the political, technological and economic arenas. From quiet, deliberate strategy to real-time crisis response, Aviv helps organizations and individuals say the right thing, the right way, at the right moment.
