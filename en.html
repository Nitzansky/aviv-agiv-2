Nice to meet you — I'm Aviv Agiv.

For more than a decade I've worked in media consulting and public relations, guiding companies, brands and public figures to the forefront of the media stage — building their reputations and advancing their business and strategic goals.

About

Proactive PR.

I believe in proactive public relations. Not just spotting the stories that already exist, but creating media interest from nothing — finding the right angle and bringing every client to the stage and the audience that fit them.

Beyond generating exposure, I specialize in leading media and public campaigns, mobilizing public opinion, and pulling the right levers of influence to advance interests and achieve goals.

My path

I began my career in the public sector and later moved to the private sector. Over the years I've worked with companies and organizations across a wide range of industries, and built deep, close relationships with reporters and editors at Israel's leading media outlets.

What I offer

My services include public relations, media consulting and strategy, public and media campaigns, government relations, strategic connections, personal branding, influencer partnerships and crisis management.

Personal approach

Every client gets end-to-end, personal support tailored to their needs and goals — with my direct involvement at every stage, constant initiative and full commitment to results.

Aviv Awakens

Alongside my work, I create and host the podcast "Aviv Awakens," where I sit down with leading figures from Israel's media, business and influence worlds.
