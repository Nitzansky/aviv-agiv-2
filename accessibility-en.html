Accessibility Statement

Aviv Agiv is committed to providing an equitable experience for all visitors and to improving the usability of this website for people with disabilities. We invest ongoing effort in making the site accessible, in the belief that everyone deserves to browse the web with dignity, independence and ease.

Conformance level

This website was built to conform with the W3C's WCAG 2.2 guidelines at Level AA, and in line with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations and Israeli Standard IS 5568.

Accessibility features

  • Semantic structure and full keyboard operability.
  • Compatibility with screen readers (e.g. NVDA, VoiceOver).
  • Sufficient colour contrast and clearly visible keyboard focus.
  • Alternative text for images and graphics, with a logical heading order.
  • Accessible forms with labels, required-field indicators and clear error messages.
  • Responsive layout (desktop, tablet and mobile) with full right-to-left (RTL) support.
  • Reduced animation honouring the operating-system prefers-reduced-motion preference.

The accessibility widget

Every page includes an accessibility button (a person icon) in the corner of the screen, opening a menu that lets you personalise your experience:

  • Increase and decrease text size.
  • High-contrast mode.
  • Highlight links.
  • Increase text and line spacing.
  • Switch to a readable font.
  • Pause animations and motion.
  • Enlarge the mouse cursor.
  • Reset all settings.

Your chosen preferences are saved in your browser and continue to apply as you move between pages.

Known limitations

Despite our efforts to make every component accessible, some parts based on third-party content (such as the embedded Spotify podcast player) may not be fully accessible. We work to improve continuously and welcome your feedback.
